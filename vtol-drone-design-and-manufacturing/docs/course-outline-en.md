# FPV Maker Course — Design, Build, Configuration, and Flight

**Drone Course 2**

English translation of the Arabic course description supplied by Hamza. This outline describes learning objectives and expected outcomes; it is not a record of individual flight-test results.

**Flight controller:** Pixhawk running PX4 firmware.  
**Simulation:** Mission Planner (optional, for simplified use only), as listed in the supplied course description.

## Learning objectives

- Understand the fundamental principles of vertical take-off and landing (VTOL) flight and aerodynamics.
- Design and assemble a functional VTOL aircraft airframe using plywood or lightweight materials.
- Install and configure a Pixhawk controller with PX4 firmware for hybrid flight control.
- Calibrate and test brushless motors, servos, and flight sensors.
- Develop transition logic between vertical take-off and forward-flight modes.
- Conduct safe ground and flight tests, analyse the results, and troubleshoot potential problems.

## Expected outcomes

- A fully functional three-motor VTOL aircraft capable of vertical take-off and horizontal flight.
- Practical experience integrating hardware and firmware for modern flight systems.
- Knowledge of motor coordination, transition control, and stability tuning.
- Confidence applying engineering design principles to real unmanned aircraft systems.
